Accommodation

Karlsruhe hotels are generally not too expensive and it should be easy for anyone coming to SOTM-EU to find a place that appeals to them. Almost all hotels have some ratings on TripAdvisor and are bookable through standard portals like hrs.com, hotel.com, booking.com. Plenty private accommodation can also be had through airbnb.com.

We have reserved rooms in two hotels ("Rio" and "Kübler") and a few beds at the Youth Hostel; these are shown with a pink tint () in the map below. To book these rooms you will have to contact the hotels/hostels directly, and not go through any of the web booking services. See below for the prices and how long the reservation is good for.

Note: Due to tram construction works during the conference weekend, some of the travel distances in this table had to be changed. You will get to the Europaplatz station on the tram lines E1 and E2. See "Getting There" for details.

Rio €€ 1150m Excellent medium-priced hotel and one of those that are nearest to the conference location. We have reserved 50 single rooms and 5 double rooms. Prices: single €60 (€89 on Thursday), double €72 (€99 on Thursday), plus €6 p.p. for buffet breakfast. You have to book directly with them (email, fax, or phone) to get one of the reserved rooms. Free WiFi.
